# AI Notebook - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-07-18

## Overview

AI Notebook ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our Chrome browser extension.

## Information We Collect

### 1. Information You Actively Provide
- **Account Information**: When you register/login, we collect email address and password (stored securely)
- **Feedback & Support**: Any messages or content you submit through our feedback form

### 2. Information Collected Automatically
- **Clip Metadata**: When you use the extension, we may temporarily store:
  - Page URL and hostname of clipped content
  - Clip type (AI chat vs article)
  - Platform name (DeepSeek, Doubao, Qwen, etc.)
  - Word count and turn count of conversations
  - Extension version number
- **Cloud Sync Data**: If you log in, your clips are synced to our cloud service (Xiansuan)

### 3. Information We Do NOT Collect
- We do NOT read, collect, or store the full content of your web browsing history
- We do NOT track your browsing habits
- We do NOT sell or share your personal information with advertisers

## How We Use Your Information

- **Provide Services**: To sync your clips to your account and enable sharing features
- **Improve Product**: To understand usage patterns and fix bugs
- **Customer Support**: To respond to your feedback and questions
- **Security**: To detect and prevent fraud or abuse

## Data Storage

- **Local Storage**: Clip results may be stored in Chrome's local storage for session continuity
- **Cloud Storage**: Logged-in users' clips are stored on Xiansuan servers (https://www.xiansuan.top)
- **Data Retention**: You can delete your cloud data at any time through your account settings

## Data Sharing

We do NOT sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. Your data may only be shared:
- With your explicit consent
- To comply with legal obligations
- To protect our rights and prevent fraud

## Third-Party Services

- **Xiansuan API**: Used for cloud sync, authentication, and sharing features
- **AI Models**: Optional AI summarization (if enabled) sends content to your configured API endpoint only

## Your Rights

You have the right to:
- Access your personal data
- Delete your account and associated data
- Export your data in a portable format
- Opt out of cloud sync at any time

## Children's Privacy

Our service is not intended for users under 16 years of age. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

## Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us:
- Email: support@xiansuan.top
- Website: https://www.xiansuan.top
