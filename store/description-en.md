## Full Description

**Your AI Chat Backup Expert**

AI Notebook turns your AI conversations into clean, searchable Markdown notes. Never lose valuable insights from DeepSeek, Doubao, or Qwen chats again.

### Why AI Notebook?

- **One-click backup** of entire AI conversations
- **Removes thinking chains** automatically - keep only the answers
- **Multi-turn conversations** collected completely via scroll
- **Duplicate removal** & gap detection for quality assurance
- **Export to Obsidian, Notion, Markdown or Word**

### Supported Platforms

**AI Chat:**
- DeepSeek (deepseek.com)
- Doubao (doubao.com)
- Qwen/Tongyi (qwen.ai)

**Content:**
- Zhihu (知乎)
- WeChat Official Accounts (微信公众号)
- Juejin (掘金)
- CSDN, GitHub, and 50+ other sites

### Use Cases

- Save research conversations for later reference
- Build a personal knowledge base from AI chats
- Archive important discussions before clearing chat history
- Export to Obsidian for bidirectional linking
- Share clean notes with colleagues via share codes

### Features

- **Smart deduplication**: Removes repeated messages automatically
- **Turn management**: Delete unwanted rounds before exporting
- **Cloud sync**: Save clips to your Xiansuan account
- **Share with codes**: Generate shareable links for teammates
- **Multi-format export**: Markdown, Word (.doc), Obsidian frontmatter, Notion blocks

### How It Works

1. Open a DeepSeek/Doubao/Qwen conversation
2. Click "Backup This Page"
3. Preview, edit, or delete rounds
4. Export to your preferred format

### Privacy

- All processing happens locally in your browser
- Cloud sync requires optional login
- No data sold or shared with third parties
